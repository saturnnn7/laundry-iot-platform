# UC-03 Book a slot in advance

Actor: Resident (member). Stories: US-12, US-13, US-22.

## Main flow
1. The resident picks a machine, a program and a start time on the 15-minute grid.
2. The platform computes the occupied range: program minutes + 5 load + 5 unload, rounded up to 15.
3. The platform checks the rules and that the range does not overlap any reservation on that machine.
4. The platform calls the payment port (a stub that approves in the first version).
5. The booking becomes Confirmed and BookingConfirmed is published.
6. Before start the resident may cancel for free until 15 minutes before the start; status becomes Cancelled.

## Alternatives
- Start less than 15 minutes from now: `lead-time`. More than 7 days ahead: `horizon`.
- Range overlaps another reservation or the machine is OutOfService: `slot-conflict` or `machine-unavailable`.
- Same `Idempotency-Key` repeated: the first result is returned.
- The resident does not claim the booking within 10 minutes after the start: NoShow, slot freed (UC-08 describes the claim).

## Rules
- Limits are Location config; defaults: lead 15 minutes, horizon 7 days.
- The overlap rule is enforced in the domain and by a database exclusion constraint.
